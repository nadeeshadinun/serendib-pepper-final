## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-end, single-origin agricultural brand rooted in the heritage of Sri Lankan spice cultivation. The brand narrative shifts from scientific purity and harvest precision at the top to rich, tactile earthiness in the lower narrative zones. 

The aesthetic is an editorial fusion of modern organic minimalism and tactile regional terroir:
- **Upper Zone (Purity & Origin):** High-contrast, clean laboratory-grade white canvases accented with soft ash gray, fractured pepper-corn charcoal (#121312), and deliberate botanical emerald foliage touches. It emphasizes single-origin provenance, harvest grading, and essential oil purity.
- **Lower Zone (Terroir & Direct Commerce):** Warm craft-paper beige, untreated timber-brown surfaces, and raw spice sacks. The tone grounds the product in local earth, Delkanda market heritage, and unadulterated freshness.
- **Tone & Mood:** Authoritative, uncompromisingly fresh, sensorial, and radically frictionless. 
- **Operational Reality:** Pure direct-to-consumer conversational commerce. Friction is eliminated by discarding accounts, carts, checkouts, and text inputs in favor of one-tap WhatsApp direct dispatch.

## Colors

The palette navigates from stark monocentric charcoal to botanical vitality and warm soil undertones:

- **Primary (`#111411` - Peppercorn Charcoal):** The bedrock deep black. Used for high-impact typography, precision borders, bold price identifiers, and anchor structural blocks.
- **Secondary (`#25D366` / Darker Shade `#128C7E` - Direct Emerald):** The conversational commerce driver. Reserved strictly for primary action buttons, instant WhatsApp order flows, live availability markers, and verified origin badges.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6239` - Ceylon Cinnamon & Sun-Dried Pod):** Deep warm spice tone anchoring middle and lower modules, certificates of authenticity, and heritage iconography.
- **Neutral (`#F4F1EA` - Sun-bleached Craft Paper):** Warm background base for earthy lower sections, product spec sheets, and retail pack callouts.
- **Surface Upper (`#FFFFFF` to `#F7F8F7` - Ash Gray):** The pristine upper-half hero backdrop maintaining editorial clarity.
- **Subtle Foliage Accent (`#1E4732` - Deep Piperine Green):** Micro-labels, piperine percentage callouts, and technical single-origin tags.

## Typography

The bilingual typographic framework demands harmony between contemporary Latin letterforms and classical Sinhala Unicode curves:

- **English Typography:** Uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and high-priority commerce labels, providing geometric punch, clean numerals for weights/prices, and contemporary authority. **Inter** serves all continuous technical body copy, harvest details, and spec tables for neutral legibility.
- **Bilingual Sinhala Typography:** Systematically paired with **Noto Sans Sinhala**. Line-height is adjusted (+4px to +8px over Latin counterparts) to prevent glyph clipping of Sinhala ascenders and descenders (kombuwa, ispilla, papilla).
- **Scale Strategy:** Headlines use negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) to convey density and weight like heavy stone or compressed whole spices. Price amounts (Rs.) are visually styled at heavier font weights than surrounding metadata to ensure glanceability in outdoor mobile conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid structured with strict content hierarchy:
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer canvas margin, `1.25rem` column gutter. Sticky commerce bottom bar permanently anchored to trigger WhatsApp transactions instantly.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` outer canvas margin, `1.5rem` gutter.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px`, centered with `4rem` side margins.

Content modules transition vertically:
1. **Pristine Hero:** Open, whitespace-dominated viewport with edge-to-edge subtle hairline grid lines.
2. **Product Grid:** 2-column on mobile, 3-column on desktop.
3. **Terroir & Processing Section:** Full-bleed warm sand container with alternating single-origin photo essays and bilingual certification proof.
4. **Physical Presence (Delkanda Hub):** 50/50 split embed modules for Facebook verification feed and Google Maps navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic digital dropshadows. Instead, depth is conveyed through tactile structural boundaries and atmospheric surface steps:

- **Upper Minimalist Tier:** Completely flat elevations using structural, ultra-fine 1px borders tinted in Peppercorn at 8% opacity (`rgba(17, 20, 17, 0.08)`).
- **Commerce & WhatsApp Cards:** Subtle, warm ambient drop elevation to make purchase actions tactile: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(17, 20, 17, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(17, 20, 17, 0.04)`.
- **WhatsApp Action Depth:** High-contrast elevation utilizing emerald luminescence: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`.
- **Lower Sectional Contrast:** Elevation created via raw tonal shifts—stepping from white to raw craft-sand (#F4F1EA), and finally to charred charcoal footers (#111411).

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft (0.25rem / 4px)** geometry.

- **Buttons & Action Triggers:** 4px radius (`0.25rem`). This sharp, confident corner treatment evokes high-end packaging, tea-and-spice timber crates, and botanical apothecary packaging rather than juvenile tech pill shapes.
- **Product Cards & Embed Frames:** 8px radius (`0.5rem` - `rounded-lg`). Just enough curvature to isolate embedded maps and imagery without losing architectural crispness.
- **Currency Badges & Micro-tags:** 2px radius or crisp right angles for an industrial, graded stamp feel.
